# Hostel rules 2027-28

Synthetic test data for the AdmitAI prototype. Owner: Student Affairs.

## Rooms

First-year students share triple rooms; senior students may apply for double rooms. Every room has a bed, study table, chair and wardrobe for each student. Wi-Fi is available in all hostel blocks.

## Mess

The hostel mess serves breakfast, lunch, evening snacks and dinner. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian food is available. The mess charge is included in the hostel fee.

## Timings and safety

Hostel gates close at 21:30. Wardens live on campus, and security staff and CCTV cover every block around the clock. Visitors are allowed in the visitors' room between 16:00 and 19:00.

## Medical care

A doctor visits the campus health centre every day, and an ambulance is available at all times.
