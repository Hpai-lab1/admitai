# Demo College of Engineering: Prospectus 2027-28

All information in this prospectus is synthetic test data for the AdmitAI prototype.

## About the college

Demo College of Engineering is a private engineering college affiliated to the state technical university. The campus is 40 acres and has separate academic, hostel and sports blocks.

## Programmes offered

Undergraduate: B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, Mechanical Engineering and Civil Engineering. Each B.Tech programme runs for four years (eight semesters).

Postgraduate: M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (two years) and MBA (two years).

## Eligibility

For B.Tech, candidates must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics with at least 50% marks (45% for reserved categories) and hold a valid rank in the state entrance test or JEE Main.

For M.Tech, a B.E. or B.Tech in a related branch with at least 50% is required; a GATE score is preferred. For the MBA, any bachelor's degree with at least 50% and a valid CAT, MAT or PGCET score is required.

## Lateral entry

Diploma holders with at least 45% marks in a related branch can join the B.Tech programme directly in the second year (third semester) through the state lateral-entry test. Seats are limited to 10% of the intake.

## Admission process

1. Apply online on the admissions portal and pay the application fee.
2. Upload your marks cards, entrance test rank card, transfer certificate and a photo ID.
3. Attend counselling on the date given in your admission letter.
4. Pay the first-year fee within seven days of the seat allotment to confirm your seat.

You receive an e-mail at each step. Original documents are verified at the time of joining.

## Documents required

Class 10 and Class 12 marks cards, entrance test rank card, transfer certificate, migration certificate (for students from other boards), category certificate if applicable, Aadhaar card and four passport-size photographs.

## Scholarships

Merit scholarships cover part of the tuition fee for students with high entrance test ranks. Need-based fee concessions are available for families with an annual income below the limit set by the scholarship committee. Students can also apply for state and central government scholarships through the National Scholarship Portal; the scholarship cell helps with the application.

## Education loans

The college has tie-ups with partner banks for education loans. The admissions office issues the fee structure letter and bonafide certificate the bank needs.

## Hostel and transport

Separate hostels for men and women are available on campus. Rooms are allotted after admission on a first-come basis. College buses run on 12 routes across the city.

## Placements

The training and placement cell runs aptitude and interview training from the third year. Recruiters from IT services, product, core engineering and consulting companies visit the campus every year.

## Refunds and cancellations

Refunds on cancellation of admission follow the state university's refund rules. Requests are handled by the admissions office, not by the online assistant.
