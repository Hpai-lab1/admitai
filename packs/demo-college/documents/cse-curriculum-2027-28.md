# B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering: Curriculum 2027-28

## Structure

The programme has 160 credits over eight semesters. The first year covers mathematics, physics, programming in C and Python, and engineering drawing.

## Core subjects

Data structures, algorithms, discrete mathematics, computer organisation, operating systems, database management systems, computer networks, theory of computation, compiler design and software engineering.

## Electives

From the fifth semester students choose electives in machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, cloud computing, cyber security, blockchain and the internet of things. Students may take a minor in data science.

## Labs

The department has a programming lab, a networks lab, an AI and machine learning lab with GPU workstations, and an IoT lab. Labs are open until 20:00 on weekdays.

## Projects and internships

Students complete a mini project in the sixth semester and a major project in the eighth semester. An internship of at least eight weeks is required before the final year.

## Higher studies and careers

Graduates work as software engineers, data analysts, machine learning engineers and network engineers, or go on to M.Tech, MS or MBA programmes. The department helps with GATE and GRE preparation.
