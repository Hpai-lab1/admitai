# B.Tech Mechanical Engineering: Curriculum 2027-28

Synthetic test data for the AdmitAI prototype. Owner: Mechanical department.

## Core subjects

Engineering mechanics, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, strength of materials, manufacturing processes, machine design, heat transfer and dynamics of machinery.

## Electives

Electives include automobile engineering, robotics, additive manufacturing, renewable energy systems and finite element analysis.

## Labs and workshop

The department has a central workshop with lathe, milling and welding sections, a CNC lab, a thermal engineering lab and a 3D printing lab. Workshop sessions run from 09:00 to 13:00.

## Careers

Graduates work in automotive, manufacturing, energy and design companies, or continue to M.Tech or MS programmes.
