# Riverside College of Commerce and Management: Prospectus 2027-28

Synthetic test data for the AdmitAI prototype.

## About the college

Riverside College is a commerce and management college in the city centre, close to the metro station. It has no hostel; the college office keeps a list of nearby paying-guest accommodation.

## Admission process

Apply online, upload your Class 10 and Class 12 marks cards, and pay the application fee. Shortlisted MBA applicants attend a group discussion and interview. Seats are confirmed when the first-year fee is paid.

## Scholarships

Students with more than 90% in Class 12 receive a merit scholarship of 25% of the tuition fee in the first year. The college also helps students apply for state government scholarships.

## Internships and placements

BBA and MBA students complete a summer internship. The placement cell invites banks, audit firms and retail companies to the campus every year.

## Library and labs

The library is open from 08:30 to 19:00 on weekdays. The computer lab has accounting and analytics software for commerce students.
